01 // OVERVIEW

Introduction.

Third-year Mechanical Engineering student at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology, working at the intersection of vehicle aerodynamics and biomimetic fluid design. Most of my research runs through ANSYS Fluent — steady-state RANS with k-ω SST closure — alongside mechanical design work for Team Zenith's Mars rover. Currently preparing for graduate study abroad, targeting TU Munich and RWTH Aachen.
